I build for desktop and mobile browsers, professionally and in my spare time, and I'm incredibly fortunate that my passion became my career. I care about the craft on the surface and the craft beneath it — semantics, accessibility, and a web that works for everyone.

Off the clock you'll find me telling a story at The Moth, onstage in community theatre, building with Lego, tinkering with hardware and VR, or traveling the world with my wife Olivia. I'm also the father of two beautiful kids.

I'm currently Tech Lead for Shared User Experience at Elastic. Before that: Facebook and Meta, Cerner, and a long tail of startups and clients — always looking for the next thing worth building.
