01 — Who I am

I live for experiences. I craft them for a living, and I chase them for the fun of it.

I'm an engineering lead and user-interface engineer with a demonstrated love for HTML, CSS and JavaScript, React and its ecosystem, and the unglamorous craft underneath — semantics, web agnostics, and accessibility. I'm as comfortable as a designer as an engineer, advising on the technical and the aesthetic in the same breath.

I'm currently Tech Lead for Shared User Experience at Elastic, working from home in Hamilton, Ontario. Before that: Facebook and Meta, Cerner, and a long tail of startups and clients. Off the clock you'll find me telling a story at The Moth, onstage in community theatre, building with Lego, or traveling the world with my wife Olivia.
